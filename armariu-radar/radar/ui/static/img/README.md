# Hero photo (optional)

Put a landscape photograph here named `hero.jpg` (about 2400 px wide, under 600 KB) and the page
fades it in over the animated skyline, with a slow zoom. Without it, the animated skyline shows on
its own. Use a photo you have the rights to (your own, or one under a free licence such as
Unsplash's), and do not commit anything you may not redistribute.
