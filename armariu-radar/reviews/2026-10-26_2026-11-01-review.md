## Data problems

1. **No count mismatches.** Tier counts add up to 2669 (4 + 227 + 1276 + 163 + 999). Category counts also add up to 2669. Tier B shows 50 rows plus "177 more" = 227. Satisfied charges show 50 plus "286 more" = 336, which matches the summary. I could not count the whole CSV by tier with the tools available, but a search for tier A and B rows gives Tier A = 4 rows, and Tier B 179 rows were classified by seed.
2. **Parent firm wrong or doubtful:** "Atempo Capital" should be "Atempo Growth" for Atempo Growth II. See flags.
3. **Duplicate-looking rows.** Several borrowers have repeated rows with identical chargee but different filing URLs (for example POLARDC SERVICES LTD, ANCHOR ABS 2026-1 PLC, NOVASTAR ENERGY HOLDINGS LIMITED with 5 rows). These are separate charge filings, not duplicate rows, but they inflate counts of "deals". Tier A/B counts are charges, not deals or lenders.
4. **Same lender spelled two ways.** "Fleet Mortgages Limited" and "Fleet Mortgage Limited" (SINGLETON MANAGEMENT LTD, Tier C). Likely a filing typo, and the second spelling may not match the lender rule.
5. **Chargee name typos from filings** ("Secutiry", "Lendlnvest") may break name matching to `lenders.csv`.
6. **Tier A has `All-assets` blank for two of four rows** (Fasanara, one Ares row). That is only a flag in the CSV (no floating charge flag), not an error.
7. **No empty borrower names and no wrong-looking dates found** in the rows I read (created dates are before delivered dates; created 2026-10-14 to 2026-10-28). SC854112 (Scottish company) has a created date of 2026-10-20 and was delivered 2026-10-28, which is plausible.
8. **Seed Tier A includes Ares as security agent.** Under the owner decision, a bank as agent is Tier B; Ares is not a bank, so Tier A is consistent. No flag.
9. I did not find banks in Tier A, and found no individuals treated as private credit in the rows I read.
